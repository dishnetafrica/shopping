DEPLOY — Owner cash actions auto-approve (v45)
==============================================
Owner/manager-created entries now post as APPROVED immediately (no more pending on your
own conversions, transfers, capital, cashbook entries and purchases). Staff-entered
costs still follow your cost_approval setting (pending -> Cost Approvals).

WHAT CHANGED (status logic)
  - Cashbook Record entry: owner/manager -> approved; staff -> pending (if approval on).
  - Convert currency  -> approved (owner/manager-gated action).
  - Move cash          -> approved (owner/manager-gated action).
  - Purchases (Book Cost / Buy List): owner/manager -> approved; staff -> pending.
  - Field app "I spent": owner/manager -> approved; staff -> pending (still reviewable).
  - Unchanged: Give cash (owner-only, approved), He received / receipts (approved),
    Settle (approved).

RESULT
  Your own conversions/capital/transfers stop landing in "pending" — they're Done at once.
  Staff-entered costs still route through Cost Approvals so you keep control there.

WHERE APPROVE/REJECT LIVES (unchanged, already deployed)
  Seller sidebar -> "Cost Approvals" (or mycloudbss.com/panel/approvals). Tabs:
  Pending / Approved / Rejected, with Approve & Reject buttons per row. Owner/manager/
  accounts only. It lists every pending entry (purchases, receipts, cashbook, etc.).

FILES (folders match your repo paths)
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/TradeCashbookController.php   (owner-aware status; convert/move)
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/OrderCostController.php       (purchases owner-aware)
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/StaffCashController.php       (field spend owner-aware)
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/PaymentController.php
  routes/web.php
  resources/panel/trade_cashbook.html  staff_cash.html  my_cash.html  payments.html  app_home.html
  seller.html

UPLOAD
  Unzip -> GitHub Add file -> Upload files -> drag contents (app/ routes/ resources/
  AND seller.html) -> Commit -> EasyPanel Rebuild (NOT Restart). No migration.
  Hard-refresh (Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+R).

VERIFY
  As owner: Cashbook -> Convert or Move -> the two rows show "Done" (not pending)
  straight away. Book a purchase as owner -> approved. (Staff-created costs still show
  pending in Cost Approvals.)

NOTE
  Entries you already created while approval was on stay pending until you approve them
  once in Cost Approvals; this change only affects new entries.
