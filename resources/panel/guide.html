How your shop works

A quick guide to what each part of the panel and the WhatsApp bot does. Search below, or scroll through.

🤖 Your WhatsApp salesperson

The bot answers customers on WhatsApp for you — it shares your catalogue, quotes prices from your product list, takes orders, and sends quotations, around the clock.

  • It only quotes prices that exist in your products — it never invents a price or a discount.
  • If it isn't sure of stock, it says “I’ll confirm” instead of guessing (see Stock counts).
  • You can step in any time — when you reply by hand the bot steps back (see Taking over a chat).

📋 Today — what needs you

Open: Today

Your first screen each morning. It lists the things that need action now — each with the reason and one button.

  • Orders past their response time, quotations awaiting a reply, deliveries to confirm.
  • Customers likely to reorder, products that need a stock count, and any failed quote sends.
  • Tap a card to jump straight to it. When nothing’s pending it shows “All clear”.

📦 Orders & statuses

Open: Orders

Each order moves through statuses (New → Accepted → … → Delivered → Customer confirmed). Update the status as you work it; the customer is notified at the right steps.

Orders left too long in one status appear on Today so nothing slips.

📄 Quotations

Open: Quotations

The bot can send a customer a price quote as a PDF. In the panel, tap View on any quote to:

  • See the line items and totals; edit quantities or prices (this rebuilds the PDF, same quote number).
  • Resend it, Convert it to an order, or Archive it.
  • Accepted/converted quotes are read-only — make a new quote to change them.

📊 Stock counts

Open: Today → Count, or Stock count

Record how much you have of each product. A counted item tells customers a real number (“17 available”); an uncounted one makes the bot say “I’ll confirm”.

  • Type the quantity, press Save — that’s it.
  • A count stays exact for 12 hours, then “usually available”, then “unknown” after 3 days. Recount fast movers.
  • Use “Needs a count only” to see what’s gone stale.

🔁 Lead follow-up

Turn on: Settings → Lead follow-up

If a customer asks something then goes quiet, the bot can send your catalogue once after a few minutes to win them back.

  • Tick the box to enable; set how many minutes to wait and an optional catalogue link.
  • It follows up once, never while you’re handling the chat, and only with warm leads (unless you widen it).

👥 Customers

Open: Customers

Profiles for people who message you — name, notes, and pricing details. Used to personalise quotes and follow-ups, and to spot who’s due to reorder.

🛒 Products & catalogue

Open: Products

Your product list powers everything — the bot’s answers, prices, and quotes.

  • Add products one by one, or import in bulk (Products → Import) using a CSV/ZIP.
  • Keep names clear and prices current — the bot quotes exactly what’s here.

🚚 Delivery confirmation

When an order is delivered, the customer gets a link to confirm receipt and rate it. If they don’t, the bot sends one polite reminder. Unconfirmed deliveries show on Today.

✋ Taking over a chat

When you reply to a customer by hand, the bot steps back so you don’t both answer.

  • A quick “hi” to wake a silent bot pauses it only briefly — it resumes for the next question.
  • A real reply hands the chat to you; the bot resumes automatically once you’re done.

⚙️ Settings

Open: Settings

Your shop details, pricing and currency, delivery fees, bot toggles (product photos, item suggestions), and Lead follow-up.

Bot toggles apply instantly; other fields need the Save button.

💬 Need a hand?

If something looks wrong or you’re unsure, contact your CloudBSS account manager — note the order or quote number and what you expected to happen.
