FAMILY SHOPPERS / CloudBSS — PAYABLES OVERSTATEMENT FIX
======================================================

BUG
  The dashboard "you owe", working-capital, and cash-runway figures computed supplier payables
  as (native_amount - paid_native). But when you pay a credit bill via "Pay supplier", CloudBSS
  creates a SEPARATE order_cost row (stage=supplier_payment) and never updates paid_native on the
  bill. So those screens counted NONE of your payments and showed GROSS bills.
  Example: Nura bhai showed $5,667.03 owed instead of the true $538.44 (overstated by 18,990,000 UGX
  — exactly the payments being ignored). Profit was NOT affected (it is cash-basis and excludes
  credit bills); only the balance-sheet "you owe" figures were wrong.

FIX
  New  app/Services/Finance/PayablesCalculator.php
       One source of truth: nets credit bills against supplier_payment rows (like
       SupplierController::owedBySupplierUsd), applies payments oldest-first for ageing, floors
       overpaid suppliers at 0. Matches the supplier statement to the penny.
  Edit app/Http/Controllers/Panel/PanelApiController.php   (dashboard payable + ageing)
  Edit app/Capability/Shared/MoneyTimingCapability.php     (payable / runway)
  Test tests/Feature/PayablesCalculatorTest.php            (regression guard)

  These are computation-only changes (no data writes, no migration). Existing data is untouched;
  the numbers simply render correctly.

DEPLOY
  Copy the app/ and tests/ trees over your repo and deploy. Then hard-refresh the panel.
  Verify:  php artisan test --filter PayablesCalculatorTest

REMAINING (lower priority, optional)
  - PurchaseOrderCapability / PurchasesExport still show a single bill's "owed" from paid_native,
    which reads as unpaid even when the account is settled (payments aren't linked to specific bills).
    The proper long-term fix is to allocate supplier payments to bills (update paid_native) at
    payment time; that would make every paid_native-based calc correct at the source.
