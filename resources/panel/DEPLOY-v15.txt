DEPLOY — Buy List: handle status up/down + undo bought (v15)
===========================================================
Two safety additions so bought-marks never go stale when order status moves both
ways:

1. AUTO-CLEAR on revert. If an order drops back to New / Cancelled / Returned, its
   bought-marks are cleared automatically on the next Buy List load. So if it's
   later re-confirmed, its items correctly re-appear to buy.
   (Only these three statuses clear. Delivered/Out-for-delivery keep their marks;
    orders merely missing from the feed are NOT touched, to avoid false clears.)

2. MANUAL UNDO. A new "Bought — tap Undo to put back" section lists items marked
   bought (with photo). Undo clears that mark and returns the item to the to-buy
   list. Fixes accidental "Buy & record" taps.

Status up/down was already handled (the list recomputes live from current status
every load): New->Confirmed shows; Confirmed<->Packed shows; ->Out-for-delivery/
Delivered/Cancelled drops; reverting re-shows. This just closes the stale-tick gap.
