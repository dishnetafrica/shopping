DEPLOY — Phase 1: Double-Entry Ledger foundation (v46)
======================================================
This is the first phase of turning the cashbook into a real accounting engine, per the
CFO audit. It is ADDITIVE and SAFE: it only creates three new tables and DERIVES a proper
double-entry general ledger from your existing order_costs. It does NOT change how the
cashbook writes today — your live screens are untouched.

*** RUN THE BACKFILL ON A SNAPSHOT/STAGING COPY FIRST if you can. It only writes to the
new ledger_* tables, but always prudent for accounting. ***

WHAT IT ADDS
  - Tables: ledger_accounts (chart of accounts), ledger_journals, ledger_lines (the GL).
  - LedgerService: seeds a standard chart, maps every cash event to a BALANCED journal
    (Debit=Credit enforced; throws if not), and backfills the ledger from order_costs.
  - Command: `php artisan ledger:backfill {tenantId?}`  (safe to re-run; it regenerates).
  - Reports page /panel/ledger: Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss (USD),
    with a "Rebuild ledger" button. Owner/manager/accounts only.
  - Seller sidebar: "Financial Statements" link; app-home "Statements" tile.

WHAT IT PROVES / SURFACES (tested on your real data)
  - Trial Balance BALANCES (total debits = total credits).
  - Owner's Capital now appears (the working-capital/opening entries that had no equity
    side before).
  - FX conversions net through an "FX Clearing" account; any residual there = an
    unreconciled/one-legged conversion (e.g. the old manual CB-0012) flagged for you.
  - Accounts Receivable shows the accrual gap: customer receipts with no matching sales
    invoice (Phase 2/3 will add invoicing/AR properly).

DEPLOY
  1. Unzip -> GitHub Upload files (paths preserved) -> Commit -> EasyPanel Rebuild.
  2. Run the migration:   php artisan migrate
  3. Build the ledger:    php artisan ledger:backfill          (or use the Rebuild button)
  4. Open Financial Statements (sidebar) -> check the Trial Balance says "In balance".

IMPORTANT — WHAT THIS IS AND ISN'T
  This is a DERIVED, read-only management ledger (functional currency USD). It is not yet
  audited financial statements. It does not change your cashbook. FX gain/loss recognition,
  inventory/COGS, tax, immutability (void/reversal), and write-time posting come in
  Phase 2 & 3. The point of Phase 1 is a balanced foundation that makes the gaps visible.
