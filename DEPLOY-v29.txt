DEPLOY — Customer 360 (v29)
===========================
Click a customer -> see everything: their orders, payments, balance owed, advances
held, and an activity timeline. Reachable from a Customers list AND by tapping a
customer's name on the Payments page.

FILES CHANGED
  NEW  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/CustomerController.php
  NEW  resources/panel/customers360.html
  CHG  routes/web.php                     (+/panel/customers360)
  CHG  resources/panel/app_home.html      (+Customers tile, everyone)
  CHG  resources/panel/payments.html      (customer name -> customer 360)

WHAT IT SHOWS
  Customers LIST: every customer (from orders + payments), searchable, sorted by who
    owes the most; each row shows order count and outstanding (red = owes, green =
    in credit). Tap a customer -> their 360.
  Customer 360: header (name, phones, WhatsApp), four money cards -
    - Invoiced (delivered)  = sum of their DELIVERED orders (USD)
    - Payments received     = sum of their payments (USD)
    - Outstanding           = Invoiced (delivered) - Payments received   (your rule)
                              (negative shows as "in credit")
    - Advances held         = their unapplied advance payments
    then Orders (with status), Payments (with receipt), and an Activity timeline
    merging orders placed / delivered / cancelled + payments.
  Verified: Yohesh $100 delivered - $90 received = $10 owed, $30 advance held; a
    fully-settled customer shows $0.

  100% client-side (reads /papi/orders + /papi/payments); no new backend data, no
  migration. Access = anyone with the trading panel.
