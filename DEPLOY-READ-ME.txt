DEPLOY — All-in-one: Move cash between people (v44)
===================================================
Adds "⇆ Move cash" to the Cashbook, completing the internal cash tools. Hand cash from
one person to another (same currency) — records it OUT of the giver's hand and IN to the
receiver's hand in ONE transaction. Owner/manager only.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
  - Cashbook toolbar: "⇆ Move cash" button (owner/manager). Form:
      From (giver) -> To (receiver) + Amount + Currency + Note + Date
    Live preview "Bhavin -> Jayesh · UGX 2,000,000 moves hands". One click writes both
    legs (cash_out from giver, cash_in to receiver), same currency, tagged "Transfer".
  - Backend: POST /papi/trade-cashbook/transfer (single DB transaction).
  - Giver's cash in hand goes down, receiver's goes up; company total for that currency
    is unchanged (it just moved between people).

  Example: Bhavin hands Jayesh UGX 2,000,000 -> Bhavin UGX -2,000,000, Jayesh +2,000,000.

FULL CASH TOOLKIT NOW (all in the Cashbook, owner/manager)

  (plus Staff Cash, My Cash field app, receipts with order+receiver+edit+delete)
