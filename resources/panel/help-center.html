🧭 How the system fits together

There are two halves. The WhatsApp assistant talks to customers day and night — prices, catalogues, quotations, orders. The panel (what you are in now) is where the team works: the till, orders, stock, money and settings. Everything both halves do lands in the same place, so a sale at the till and a sale on WhatsApp end up in the same stock ledger, the same invoices, the same cashbook.

Use the left menu to move around. Each person's login only shows the menus their role needs.

"I can't see a menu the trainer mentioned." That is normal, not broken — your role does not include it. The owner controls this per person in Settings → Team & Roles. If a whole group is missing for everyone, that module is switched off for the business.

📅 A good daily rhythm

  1. Morning: the owner/manager opens Inventory Trust and clears the day's check queue (5 minutes). Cashier opens the POS Till.
  2. All day: WhatsApp orders flow in by themselves; the till rings walk-in sales; deliveries go out with a signed delivery note.
  3. Evening: cashier taps TODAY on the till and matches the total against the cash drawer. Accounts checks Cashbook.
Sales

🛒 POS Till — ring up a sale

Open: Menu → Sales → POS Till

  1. Add items. Scan the barcode (the scanner types it and presses Enter for you — the item jumps into the cart), or type part of the name, or tap the product tiles. Category chips narrow the grid.
  2. Check the cart. Use − / + or type the quantity (decimals are fine for weighed items). ✕ removes a line. Discount box takes an amount off the whole sale.
  3. Take the money. CASH: tap the note the customer gave (or type it) — the screen shows the change, and repeats it after saving so you can't forget. MOMO / CARD: confirm once the money has arrived. CREDIT: only with the customer's name or phone — the sale is saved as an unpaid invoice to collect later.
  4. Receipt. Tap 🖨 Print receipt — it prints as a small 80 mm slip on a thermal printer.
Scanner tip: you never need to click into the search box — start scanning or typing anywhere and the box catches it.
Keyboard = speed: F5 cash · F6 mobile money · F7 card · F8 credit · F9 split · F4 hold · F3 discount · Enter confirms · Esc cancels. A whole sale needs zero mouse clicks.
Customer forgot an item? Press ⏸ Hold (F4) — the sale is parked, you serve the next customer, then tap ▤ Held to bring it back exactly as it was. Held sales stay on this till (even after closing the browser) until recalled.
Customer pays with two methods? Use ⇄ SPLIT (F9) — type how much cash, how much mobile money, how much card. If something remains, it goes on credit automatically (name or phone required) and the invoice shows the balance.
Internet down? Keep selling. The till shows an orange ⚡ OFFLINE chip but scanning, selling and receipt printing keep working — sales are saved on the till and sync to the system by themselves when the connection returns (the receipt says PENDING SYNC until then, and the chip counts what's waiting). Never close the sale twice; it can never duplicate.
Lost receipt? Open TODAY → tap 🖨 next to the sale — it prints again, marked REPRINT.
Who is buying? Tap the 👤 Walk-in customer chip above the cart to attach a customer — search by phone or name, or ＋ New customer to add one on the spot (name, phone, address). The sale, receipt, credit fields and quotations all pick them up automatically, and the customer is filed into Customers with their purchase history. Tap ✕ on the chip to go back to walk-in; every new sale starts as walk-in again.
Customer wants a price on paper, not a sale? Build the cart → tap 📝 Quote → name/phone → a numbered quotation PDF opens (same format the WhatsApp assistant sends — one quote system). When they come back to buy: TODAY → Quotations tab → ⤵ Load puts the quoted items straight into the cart at the quoted prices — take the money as normal. The TODAY panel now has three tabs: Sales (reprint 🖨, return ↩), Quotations (print, load), Drafts / Held (recall parked carts).
Your shift = your drawer. Start the day with ▶ Open shift (count the float first!). At closing, TODAY → Close shift: count the notes into the sheet (15 × 1000…), the system compares against float + your cash sales and prints a signed cash-up slip. A zero variance protects YOU — it is proof your drawer was right.
Big discount? Above the till limit the screen asks for the manager PIN — call the manager; the server checks the PIN, so there is no way around it. The owner sets the limit and PIN in Settings → Payments → POS till.
⚡ chip has a number? Tap it — it lists every sale still waiting to sync, with a RETRY NOW button. A sale marked FAILED needs the owner's eyes.
Second screen for the customer: tap 🖥 on the till → a customer-facing window opens — drag it to the second monitor and press F11. It shows each item as you scan, the running total, the cash received and the change, and a thank-you screen after payment. No setup, no extra computer — it mirrors this till automatically.
Customer brings something back? Tap ↩ (top bar, or next to the sale in TODAY) → find the sale by receipt number or phone → pick the items and quantities → choose CASH BACK, MOMO or STORE CREDIT. The system checks nobody returns more than was bought, asks for the manager PIN, puts the stock back on the shelf, issues a numbered credit note, and prints a return slip the customer signs. Store credit sticks to the customer's phone number. Cash refunds come out of YOUR drawer — your shift's expected cash adjusts automatically. Returns need internet (the sale must be verified). An exchange = process the return, then ring the new items as a normal sale.
"It let me sell an item marked out of stock." Deliberate. The till never blocks a customer standing at the counter — the stock number simply goes negative and the morning Trust queue asks someone to check that item. Sell first, investigate later.
Credit sales: never press CREDIT without a real name or phone number — that money has no owner otherwise. Collection happens later from the order/invoice with Record payment.

🧾 Orders — every sale in one register

Open: Menu → Sales → Orders

All orders land here no matter where they were born — WhatsApp bot, the till (channel "pos"), or Create order typed by you. The margin view shows what each order earns after costs.

  • Move the order along as it really happens; when it becomes Delivered, stock is deducted and the invoice is issued automatically. Cancel returns the stock.
  • Record payment whenever money arrives — part payments are fine; the invoice tracks the balance.
  • Delivery note: open the order → ⋯ more → Print delivery note. It shows quantities only (never prices), with signature boxes — the customer signs, the driver brings it back. Keep the signed copy.
"The bot said a customer confirmed a quotation." That is your cue: the bot never converts by itself. Open the quotation and turn it into an order — until you do, no stock moves and no invoice exists.
Every status change messages the customer in trade language ("your order is being packed at our store", "on the way to you"). Want your own wording for a stage? Ask support to set a status message template — placeholders {name}, {order_no}, {business} are filled automatically, and the customer is always greeted by first name, never "HOTEL DE JUBA".
Revising a quotation? When you change prices or items, the customer automatically gets the full updated list on WhatsApp — every line with its amount, a "What changed" note, the new total, the fresh PDF and their review link. You never need to re-type the quotation in a chat. The same works in reverse: when the customer edits on their link, YOUR alert shows what they changed and what they asked for. And a revised quotation converts to an order exactly like a fresh one — the Convert button never disappears while the quote is open.

👥 Customers — who buys, who owes

Open: Menu → Sales → Customers

One card per customer: their orders, invoices, payments and balance. Customers are filed automatically from every sale (till and WhatsApp), so you rarely create one by hand. Use it before calling anyone about money — the balance and history are already there.

Chasing money? Open the customer and use the ⋯ menu at the top right. Send statement on WhatsApp puts the actual statement PDF in their hand, and each line now says what the charge was for — Aluminium Foil ×5, Cling Film ×5 — not just an invoice number. Download statement (PDF) gives you the same document to print or attach yourself.

A customer who owes nothing can still be sent one. People ask for a statement to close their own books, not only because they are behind. The only customer the system refuses is one with no invoices at all — there would be nothing on the page.

💰 Credit on account — when a customer pays too much

Open: Menu → Sales → Customers → the customer

A customer with a USD 170 bill hands over USD 200 and says keep the rest. Record the USD 200 exactly as it was handed over — the system settles the invoice with USD 170 and holds the other USD 30 as credit on account. You do not need to do anything differently, and you should never round the payment down to make it fit.

That held money shows up in three places: as Credit we hold on the customer's record, as its own line on their statement, and on the receipt they get on WhatsApp — which states the full USD 200 they handed over, then says the USD 30 is being held. It is not lost and it is not a discount. It is still their money and the books treat it that way.

Spending it. Next time that customer has an invoice with something owing, open the invoice and the top button reads Use credit. One tap puts the held money against that invoice. If they hold more than the invoice needs, only what is needed is used and the rest stays held.

Credit is held in the currency it was paid in. USD credit goes against USD invoices, UGX against UGX. The button will not offer to spend UGX credit on a USD invoice, because that is an exchange-rate decision and it should be somebody's choice, not a side effect of a button.

📄 Invoices — the numbered money record

Open: Menu → Sales → Invoices

  • Invoices are issued automatically when an order is delivered (numbered like INV-2026-0001). You can also Issue one from an order manually.
  • Record payment here or on the order — same money, same place. Status moves Paid / Part-paid by itself.
  • Send WhatsApps the PDF straight to the customer. Mistake on an invoice? Issue credit note — never delete.
Purchases

🛒 Buying stock — list → purchase → shelf

Open: Menu → Purchases

  • Buy List — the shared shopping list. Anyone who spots something running low adds it here, so nothing lives in someone's head.
  • Purchases → New Purchase — record what was actually bought and what it cost. This is where cost prices (and later margins) come from, so type real numbers.
  • Suppliers — who you buy from; Suppliers We Owe shows unpaid supplier balances so nothing is forgotten.
  • Sourcing Requests — things a customer wants that you don't stock yet.
Why does my purchase wait in Cost Approvals? Spending above the limit needs a second pair of eyes, and nobody can approve their own spend. That is protection for you, not distrust — an approved purchase can never be questioned later.
Stock

📦 Stock count — tell the system what's really on the shelf

Open: Menu → Stock → Inventory · or the count link sent to you on WhatsApp

  1. Count what is physically there — do not peek at the system number first, and never copy it.
  2. Type the counted quantity and Save. A difference is fine — that is exactly what the count is for.
Counts are how the system learns to trust the shelf. An honest "short by 3" today saves hours of searching at month end. Differences go to the Trust queue for a calm check — they are not an automatic blame.

🛡️ Inventory Trust — the owner's 5-minute morning habit

Open: Menu → Stock → Inventory Trust (same list as the 06:45 WhatsApp digest)

The engine watches every stock movement and lines up the few items worth checking today, ranked by expected loss. The tiles in plain words: Verified stock — how much of your stock value has been counted and confirmed recently. Shrink — value that disappeared without being sold, as % of sales. Attributed — how much of that loss is explained (a stage, a process, a person). Queue precision — of the items you checked, how many found something real.

  1. Each morning, open the queue and check the listed items physically.
  2. Resolve each one: clean (all fine — raises that person's reputation), error (paperwork/process mistake — taxes nobody), or confirmed loss (type the value).
  3. Receiving: when goods arrive, staff use the WhatsApp link to confirm quantities received — that evidence is what keeps their score strong.

Shelf cameras: when a vision box arrives, plug it in with internet; a claim code appears on its screen — type it into "Claim box" here. Camera events then feed the same queue.

Say it the way the system means it: a queue item is a question, never an accusation. "Clean" answers most of them. Using it to blame people will make staff stop counting honestly — and then the numbers are worth nothing.

🚚 Consignments — the Kampala → Juba bus, box by box

Open: Menu → Inventory → Consignments — or select orders on Sales Orders and tap 🚌 Send by bus

One consignment = one bus load. It carries customer orders (the manifest) and loose market stock, and tracks custody the whole way: packed → handed to transporter → departed → arrived, with box counts at every handoff — one box missing is flagged automatically on the exact leg where it disappeared.

  1. Load it: on Sales Orders, tick the orders going on the bus → 🚌 Send by bus → create (or pick) the consignment. Add any market stock below the manifest.
  2. Pack it: in "How it is packed", add a box for each carton/bag, choose the packing (carton, plastic bag, sack…), tick whose goods go inside, add a note. This replaces typing "1 box for gurpreet, 2box - rawu,michel" in the group chat — and it prints.
  3. Paper & labels: 🖨 Print manifest (orders + box map + signatures for loading/receiving) and 🏷 Print box labels (QR per box showing whose goods and the packing).
  4. Dispatch: tap Dispatch with the bus details. The Juba agent automatically gets a WhatsApp with the box-by-box list and a no-login receive link; "Tell customers it's on the way" messages every customer once it departs.
  5. Receive in Juba: the agent opens the link on any phone — scans or ticks each box, confirms arrival with a photo, then ticks each customer as they collect (✓ Given). Each tick marks the real order Delivered — invoice and customer message happen by themselves.
Juba needs stock? The Request card on the Consignments screen sends the "please pack & send" list straight to Kampala's WhatsApp — and unsold stock carries over to the next consignment automatically.
Box count is sacred. Count at every handoff. If Juba receives 11 of 12, the system flags exactly which leg lost the box — that is your claim against the transporter, in writing.
Money

💰 Payments & Cashbook — money always has a name

Open: Menu → Money → Payments / Cashbook / Partners

Payments Received lists every payment recorded anywhere — till, order, invoice. The one rule that confuses people: when recording a payment, "received by" means whoever physically took the money, not whoever typed it in. That is why Cashbook has tabs — Cashbook (the trade ledger), Cash Position (where money sits right now), Staff Cash (each person's pocket balance to hand over), My Cash (your own).

  • Evening routine: each person's Staff Cash balance is what they hand to the safe. It should reach zero.
  • Expenses — record spending the moment it happens, with a note. P&L is only as honest as this screen.
  • Partners — money an OWNER takes out is not an expense. Each partner has a capital account: what they put in (opening / introduced) and what they take out (drawing). Record an owner's monthly money on Partners, never as Salary/Wages on the Cashbook. A drawing reduces cash and reduces that partner's capital, and leaves profit untouched — because an owner taking their own profit is not a cost of the business. Booked as a wage instead, $500 a month reads as $6,000 a year of expense that was never a cost, and the business looks less profitable than it is. Staff wages are Salary/Wages on the Cashbook — the difference is whether the person owns the business, not what you call the payment.
  • Distribute profit (Partners) shares retained profit into the capital accounts by ownership %. It moves no cash — it is what drawings are then taken against.

📊 Reports — Profit & Loss

Open: Menu → Reports

Sales minus cost of goods minus expenses, by period. If a number looks wrong here, the cause is almost always upstream: a purchase without a cost, an expense never typed in, or an order stuck in the wrong status.

WhatsApp & Messaging

🤖 The WhatsApp assistant — what it does on its own

It answers customers using your live product list — new products and price changes reach it within a minute, automatically. It quotes your delivery policy from Settings word-for-word. Ask it for a price list and it sends the catalogue; name a category ("exercise books price list") and it sends just that category's PDF. When a customer confirms an order, it prepares a quotation PDF and alerts the owner.

  • It will not attach catalogues to shipping-cost questions, spam documents (one document per few minutes), or quote the same order twice — a repeat "yes" becomes a confirmation alert to the owner instead.
  • Teach it in Settings → Business profile: FAQ, brand knowledge, and the Delivery policy box (exact wording it must use).
Minimum order quantities (MOQ): two switches must both be on for the bot to refuse small quantities. (1) Each product needs its MOQ field filled (Products → edit, or the "min order" column on import) — a minimum written only inside the product NAME enforces nothing. (2) Settings → WhatsApp & bot → Selling rules must be set to Enforced. Then a request below the minimum is rounded up, the quote line says "min order 5 — set to 5", and the AI tells the customer plainly. On Advisory (the default) the bot quotes any quantity and only mentions the minimum. The till is never blocked — a seller at the counter can always sell below MOQ deliberately.
The bot cannot finish a sale. A confirmed quotation still needs a human to convert it to an order — that step is yours.

💬 Chats — jump in whenever you want

Open: Menu → Messaging → Chats

Every conversation, live. Press Take over to reply yourself; the assistant reads what you wrote so it stays consistent when it resumes. Customers never see a difference except the answer quality you add.

From the customer's page too: open any customer record — a 💬 WhatsApp card shows their recent messages with a compose box right there. Tap ✨ AI draft and the AI writes a personalised message from what that customer is looking for (their goal, usual products) — you read it, edit it, and press Send yourself. The AI never sends anything on its own.

📣 Leads, Campaigns, Scheduled, Ads

Open: Menu → Messaging

Leads — potential customers (add or import); work them until they order. Campaigns — broadcast messages to chosen customer groups; send less, target better. Scheduled — future deliveries the bot arranged. Ads — promo material.

✨ AI Campaign (owner): pick an audience (stage, what they're looking for, how recently active) → the AI writes a DIFFERENT personalised message for each customer from their memory → every draft waits in a review list where you approve, edit or skip → sending runs slowly (about 2 a minute, daytime only, random gaps) to protect the WhatsApp number. Customers mid-deal (confirmed quotation) are skipped automatically. Nothing ever sends without your approval.
Before a broadcast, skim for customers with an open confirmed quotation — a marketing push mid-negotiation reads badly. (The ✨ AI Campaign does this check for you.)

Catalogue & Settings

📕 The PDF catalogue — how to make it beautiful

Set up in: Settings → Business profile → Branding / Catalogue / Contact

  • Logo and cover image: use Upload image… then press Save changes — uploading alone is not saved yet.
  • Cover art: a portrait image (taller than wide, e.g. 1024×1536) becomes the full front page exactly as designed. Landscape images are used as a cropped banner instead. Don't bake product counts into the art — they go stale.
  • Badges: list product names under Best sellers / Hot deals to print BEST SELLER / HOT DEAL flashes.
  • Phone & Website (Contact group) print on the cover and every footer, with a scan-to-WhatsApp QR.
  • The catalogue updates itself when products change. Customers asking about one category get that category's small PDF automatically.
First generation after many product changes takes up to a minute (it rebuilds photo thumbnails once), then it's fast and small (a few MB, not 30+).

⚙️ Settings — the five minutes that steer everything

Open: Menu → Settings

  • Business profile — name, address, TIN (prints on receipts, invoices, delivery notes); Branding & Catalogue images; Delivery policy (the exact sentence the bot and catalogue use — e.g. "All prices include free delivery within Juba…"); AI knowledge → Products the AI knows: set it above your product count or the bot literally cannot see products late in the alphabet.
  • WhatsApp — connect the number the assistant answers on.
  • Team & Roles — one login per person, menus per role. Nobody shares a login: payments and counts are recorded by name.
  • Modules — switch whole areas of the product on/off; Billing — your plan; Advanced — bot diagnostics for the owner when something looks off.
When something looks wrong

🧯 Quick answers to the usual confusions

  • "I uploaded an image but the catalogue doesn't show it." Press Save changes after uploading, then regenerate the catalogue. Cover art must be portrait to take the full page.
  • "The bot doesn't know my new product." It learns within a minute — check the product is Active with a price above zero, and that Products the AI knows (Settings → AI knowledge) is set higher than your product count.
  • "The bot didn't send the catalogue when I expected." It only sends documents when clearly asked (catalogue / price list / menu), never for shipping-cost questions, and at most one document every few minutes per chat. That's protection against spamming customers, not a fault.
  • "Customer said yes to a quotation — now what?" The owner gets one alert; open the quotation and convert it to an order. Nothing moves until a human does this.
  • "Stock shows a minus number." Something sold that the system didn't think was there. The till allows it on purpose; the Trust queue will ask someone to check that item. Count it, resolve, move on.
  • "Where is the delivery note?" Open the order → ⋯ more → Print delivery note. Quantities only, three signature boxes.
  • "A credit sale — where do I collect?" It became an unpaid invoice. When the customer pays, Record payment on the order or invoice — never just pocket-and-remember.
  • "I recorded a payment but the invoice still shows part-paid." Check the amount and who it was recorded under; part payments are normal and the balance is shown on the invoice.
  • "I can't see a menu / a button others have." Role permissions — ask the owner (Settings → Team & Roles). Not a bug.
  • Still stuck? The owner can run bot diagnostics under Settings → Advanced, and every screen's numbers can be traced back to an order, a purchase, an expense or a count — find which of those is missing and the mystery usually solves itself.
