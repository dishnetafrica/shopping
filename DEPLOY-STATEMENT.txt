DEPLOY — Partial payments on invoices, customer statement of account, 360 + self-service
========================================================================================

WHAT YOU GET
1) PARTIAL PAYMENTS on an invoice (currency-correct).
   In the Invoices register each unpaid invoice has a "💵 Pay" button. Enter what the customer
   paid (in the invoice's own currency — USD for export, UGX for local) and the invoice updates
   live: Paid goes up, Balance goes down, status flips issued → part paid → paid. The stored PDF
   is refreshed so a re-download shows the new balance. (This is the customer-billing view; your
   operational cash tracking on the order / staff-cash is unchanged.)

2) CUSTOMER STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT.
   A statement is built from a customer's non-void invoices, in the currency they were billed, and
   grouped so a South Sudan customer sees "Total outstanding: USD 380". Two ways to send it:
     - Invoices register → "📄 Statement" on any of that customer's invoices → WhatsApps them the
       full statement PDF (all invoices, paid, balance, total outstanding).
     - Customer 360 → open a customer → new "📄 Invoices & statement" section with a
       "📤 Send statement on WhatsApp" button.

3) CUSTOMER 360 — now includes invoices.
   The 360 detail page now shows the customer's invoices with paid/balance, the accurate billed
   outstanding (per currency), and the send-statement button — alongside the existing orders,
   payments and activity timeline.

4) CUSTOMER SELF-SERVICE (wired end-to-end).
   A customer logs into your shop (mycloudbss.com/<shop>) with a phone OTP and taps "My account".
   They now see, above their past orders: a big "Total outstanding" banner (per currency, e.g.
   USD 380 — or a green "No outstanding balance" when clear), and each invoice with its date, amount
   billed, amount paid and balance. So customers self-check exactly what they owe, in the currency
   they were billed. Backend (myOrders) returns it; the storefront screen (shop.html) displays it.

NOTE ON PAYMENTS (so the books stay clean)
  - "💵 Pay" here records against the INVOICE in the invoice's currency (what the customer owes on
    paper). The order-level "💵 Record Payment" on the Orders screen records operational cash in
    UGX. For export (USD) customers these are naturally different views; use the invoice Pay for the
    customer's statement/balance. If you'd like the two unified (one payment updates both), I can
    add that with an explicit UGX↔USD rate on each payment — just ask.
