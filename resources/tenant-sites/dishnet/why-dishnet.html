Real reasons

What people don't tell you about buying direct

Things we hear from customers who tried it first.

Time is the hidden cost

The money "saved" by buying direct evaporates when you're waiting 6 weeks for customs to clear, then troubleshooting setup alone, then trying to get billing right.

What customers tell us: "I should have just come to you first."

Card payments are the biggest blocker

Many South Sudan-issued cards get rejected by Starlink's payment processor. International card requirements, foreign exchange limits, and OTP verification across time zones all add up.

With us: SSP, USD cash, or local card — no foreign-payment headaches.

Warranty across continents is a nightmare

If your dish fails after a year, Starlink's process is "ship it to USA, we'll ship a replacement." Customs both ways. Cost. Time. You're offline for weeks.

With us: Bring it to Tomping, we swap it. Same day.

Local support in your language

When your internet is down and you have a video call in an hour, you don't want to email Texas and wait 8 hours. You want WhatsApp, in English or Arabic, replying in minutes.

Our hours: Support 7 AM – 10 PM. Sales/accounts in working hours.
