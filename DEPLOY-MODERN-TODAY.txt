BUILD — Modern Today (Design System v1.0 frozen) live in the app
=================================================================
The redesign you reviewed (9.2/10) is now built into the real Today screen with your LIVE data — not a
mockup. Design system is frozen (OPERATING-MODEL/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md); from here it's workflows + interactions.

WHAT'S NEW ON TODAY
  • Day-progress hero — "Remaining to collect today USD X", a progress bar, and "USD Y collected of USD Z".
    It fills as you collect and flips to "Mission complete 🎉" when nothing's left. (Real numbers: remaining
    = open invoice balances, collected = today's receipts, target = the two combined.)
  • Compact header — small date + greeting, so the hero dominates (no more oversized greeting).
  • Live sync pill — ● Synced / ● N queued / ● Offline, top-right, from the offline outbox.
  • Status + context cards — coloured avatar, a status dot (red overdue / amber due-today / green upcoming),
    the primary invoice, a human due-label ("3 days overdue", "due today", "due in 5 days") and city, then
    the amount + OUTSTANDING. Whole card taps into the customer.
  • Soft "Apple-Wallet" elevation, the one outline-icon family, and the frozen radii/type/shadow scale.

VERIFY
  1. Open the app on your phone -> Today shows the green day-progress hero and the status cards with real
     invoice/due context. Collect a payment -> reopen Today -> "collected" rises and "remaining" drops.
