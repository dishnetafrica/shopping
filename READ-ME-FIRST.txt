UPLOAD TO GITHUB — Phase 1 · Step 4: Native cart & WhatsApp checkout
====================================================================
Closes the biggest gap from the audit: there was no real cart. Now customers
can gather several parts and order them all in ONE WhatsApp message — no more
one-part-at-a-time, and no hand-off to a different system.

WHAT CHANGED (customer-facing):
  • "Add to cart" on part cards, product pages, and diagram lists (was one-off order).
  • Live cart badge (count) in the header and on the mobile bottom bar; add-to-cart toast.
  • New Cart page: line items, quantity +/- , live total, your saved bike, and your
    delivery / payment / genuine promises.
  • "Place order on WhatsApp" sends the whole basket (items, quantities, total, bike)
    in one prefilled message. Clear note: "no payment is taken online."
  • The header/bottom "Cart" now opens this native cart (was linking to the ERP shop).
  • Instant "Order now" (single part on WhatsApp) is still available on product pages.

Frontend only (resources/storefront/autoparts.html); PHP unchanged.
