DEPLOY — Customer 360 redesigned (tabbed, CRM-style) + where the statement lives
================================================================================

WHAT CHANGED (resources/panel/customers360.html only — no backend, no migration)
  The customer detail page is rebuilt into a clean, tabbed layout like a proper CRM:
    Header:  avatar · name · phone(s) · WhatsApp
    Cards:   Invoiced (delivered) · Payments received · Outstanding · Advances held
    Tabs:    Overview · Invoices · Payments · Statement · Returns / Refunds · Activity

  - OVERVIEW: outstanding balance + recent orders + recent payments + a "Send statement" button.
  - INVOICES: every invoice for the customer with billed / paid / balance (from the invoice ledger).
  - PAYMENTS: all receipts (native amount + USD).
  - STATEMENT: the full statement of account (total outstanding per currency) + "Send statement on
    WhatsApp". ← this is WHERE YOU SEE THE STATEMENT.
  - RETURNS / REFUNDS: this customer's returns/refunds, plus any store-credit available.
  - ACTIVITY: the timeline (orders placed/delivered, payments).

WHERE TO SEE A STATEMENT (three places)
  1) Customer 360 → open a customer → "Statement" tab (or the Overview "Send statement" button).
  2) Invoices register → "📄 Statement" on any of that customer's invoices.
  3) The customer themselves: shop login → "My account" shows their balance + invoices.

HOW REFUNDS & CREDIT WORK TODAY (for reference)
  - Order screen → "↩ Return / refund" lets you: give STORE CREDIT, mark a REFUND pending, or
    REDEEM existing credit. Returns are stored (ReturnRecord) and show in the new Returns tab.
  - STORE CREDIT is a running balance tracked by the customer's phone and auto-offered on their
    next order ("💳 Apply credit").
  - There is NOT yet a formal numbered "Credit note" document (like your ISP CRM's Credit notes
    tab). If you want proper numbered credit notes (CN-2026-0001, PDF, WhatsApp), say the word and
    I'll add them the same way as invoices.
