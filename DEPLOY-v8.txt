HOW IT WORKS
  - Buy List page reads /papi/orders (your native orders: items_json + status).
  - "Needs buying" window = status in {Confirmed, Packed, Out for delivery}.
  - It subtracts trade_purchase_ticks (what Jayesh marked bought) -> shows remaining,
    aggregated by item across orders, with the order numbers.
  - "Buy & record" on an item:
      1) POST /papi/buy-list/mark  (marks those order-items bought)
      2) opens /panel/book-cost?orders=<ids>&note=<item>  -> Book Purchase in SPLIT
         mode with those orders pre-ticked; Jayesh sets amount + cash/credit + supplier.
  - "Ready to pack" = CONFIRMED orders whose every item is bought (advisory flag).
  - trade_purchase_ticks is a NEW isolated table. ShopBot's orders table is never
    written to by any of this.

  Verified logic: 2 confirmed orders (foil x2+x1, cling x2) -> Buy List shows Foil 3,
  Cling 2. Mark ORD-11 fully bought -> its items leave, ORD-11 shows Ready to pack,
  ORD-12 still needs Foil 1.
