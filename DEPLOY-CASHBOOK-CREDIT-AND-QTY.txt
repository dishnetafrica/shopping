DEPLOY — Cashbook: credit purchases out + show purchase QTY (v64)
=================================================================
This ONE file replaces the v63 file — it contains BOTH improvements:

1. CREDIT PURCHASES excluded from the Cashbook (v63). A credit purchase is a payable, not a
   cash movement, so it no longer shows as a cash "Out". It lives in "Suppliers We Owe" and on
   the supplier's View. When you pay the supplier, THAT cash-out shows in the Cashbook.
   (Cash purchases and supplier payments still show normally.)

2. QUANTITY now shown on purchase lines. Purchases made via the New Purchase screen (which
   capture qty + unit cost) now read e.g.:
       CLING FILM 45CM (CARTON OF 6PC, MIN 10)  (qty 15 x UGX 49,667)
   The qty appears in the on-screen list, the CSV export, and the PDF (all read the same field).
   Older purchases booked without a qty just show the name (nothing to display).
