DEPLOY — Per-person Cashbook + export (v65)
===========================================
Adds a "person" filter to the Cashbook so you (owner/manager) can view and export ONE
person's cashbook — e.g. Bhavin only — to see exactly what's happening with their cash.

WHAT'S NEW
  - A "👥 All staff" dropdown above the entries. Pick Bhavin / Asish / Jayesh → the list,
    the balance cards, AND the running balance all switch to just that person.
  - CSV export → only that person's rows; filename includes the name
    (e.g. cashbook-Bhavin_Madlani-UGX-2026-07-09.csv).
  - PDF export → titled "Cashbook — Bhavin Madlani · UGX" with only their rows.
  - Also carried forward: credit purchases stay OUT of the cashbook, and purchase rows show
    "(qty N x <cur> <unit>)" — now in the export too.
