WHAT'S NEW ON THE CASHBOOK
  1. "Whose cash (person)" selector on the Money in / Money out form.
     - Defaults to "— me (yourname) —".
     - Owner/manager can pick another person (Bhavin / Asish / Jayesh …), so the cash leaves
       (money out) or lands in (money in) THAT person's hand — not always the logged-in user.
     - Staff who aren't owner/manager always record under themselves (safe).
     - People list comes from /papi/staff-cash (same source as everywhere else).
  2. Record a PURCHASE directly: pick category "Purchase" (or "Local Purchase") on a Money out.
     It is booked as stage 'purchase' so it counts as a purchase/COGS in P&L & the ledger,
     instead of a generic expense. Cash still leaves the chosen person's hand exactly the same.
  3. Negative-cash guard on Money out: if the chosen person doesn't have enough in that
     currency, you get a warning with the numbers and can continue or cancel (field-friendly).
