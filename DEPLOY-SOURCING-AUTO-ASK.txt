DEPLOY — Sourcing: auto-ask Jayesh on WhatsApp, remind until priced, reply-to-price
====================================================================================

WHAT YOU GET (on /panel/sourcing)
  - Post a sourcing request → the buyer (Jayesh) is messaged on WhatsApp automatically, right
    away, using your connected WhatsApp — no more tapping a wa.me link. If the request has a
    PHOTO, it's sent as the image (with the details as the caption) so Jayesh sees exactly what
    to price. Reminders stay text-only. (Needs the photo to be reachable at your public
    /storage URL, which it is by default.)
  - The system keeps nudging him every few hours (working hours) until he prices it — capped at
    6 nudges so it can never spam. Each request shows "asked 2×" etc., with a "Remind now" button.
  - Jayesh replies the price straight from WhatsApp: "12 45000" (SR id + price, optional UGX/USD/
    SSP). The request is marked PRICED instantly and he gets "✅ Saved: <item> = UGX 45,000".
    This works even if the bot is switched off (it's an internal ops reply, handled first).
  - The person who ASKED (e.g. Asish) is then WhatsApp'd automatically: "✅ Price in — <item>:
    UGX 45,000 (by Jayesh)…", so he knows without checking the screen. (Needs the requester's
    User to have a phone number saved; if not, it's simply skipped. Not sent if he priced it
    himself.) Fires whether the price came from Jayesh's reply or the panel "Add price".
  - Set the buyer once via the "⚙ Set buyer's WhatsApp" chip (name + number) — now stored on the
    server (a tenant setting), so it works for everyone, not just one browser. An old number saved
    in a browser is migrated up automatically.

HOW THE REPLY IS SAFE
  Only the configured buyer number can price by reply, and only when the message is exactly
  "<id> <price>" for a real OPEN request — so it can never catch a normal chat message. The whole
  hook is wrapped in try/catch and runs before the bot, so it never affects customer replies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FINALISE INTO CATALOGUE — the operations last mile (what to do after it's priced)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FLOW (as an ops person):
  1) KNOW: the moment Jayesh prices it, the request goes PRICED and the requester gets a
     WhatsApp. Nothing to watch.
  2) DECIDE: open the priced request — you see the cost (Jayesh's Juba price). Decide the
     Juba SELLING price = cost + your margin.
  3) FINALISE: tap "➕ Add to catalogue". A short form is prefilled — product name, cost
     (locked to Jayesh's price), suggested selling price (cost +30%, editable, shows live
     margin %), category (type-ahead from your existing categories), MOQ, unit. Tap
     "Create product".
  4) LIVE: the product is created in your catalogue with that cost + price, and the sourcing
     photo becomes its image. It is now sellable everywhere — customers/WhatsApp bot, price
     list, orders, and it can be picked in loose stock / added to a consignment for Juba.
     The request is marked done and shows "✓ IN CATALOGUE".

NEW: POST /papi/sourcing/{id}/convert  (SourcingController::convert) — creates a Product
  (name, category, price, cost_price = quote, image from the request photo, moq/unit) and
  links it back. Needs product permission (canMenu('products')); respects category scope.
  Migration 2026_07_11_000002 adds sourcing_requests.product_id.

VERIFY (finalise)
  - Price a request -> it shows "Add to catalogue" -> tap it -> set selling price -> Create.
  - The item appears under Products with cost = Jayesh's price and your selling price, and the
    request shows "✓ IN CATALOGUE". Search the bot / price list for it — it's there.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN THE KAMPALA PRICE IS TOO HIGH TO SELL (safety valve)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A priced request now has THREE choices instead of only "Add to catalogue":
  - "Ask better price"  -> re-opens the request, restarts the auto-nudges, and messages Jayesh
      "⚠️ The Juba price (UGX X) is too high to sell — can you find a better price? Target: …".
      His next "<id> <price>" reply re-prices it. This is the negotiation loop.
  - "Too high · won't stock" -> marks it NOT STOCKED with your reason (kept for the record), and
      WhatsApps the requester "🚫 Not stocking <item> — <reason>." so nothing is left hanging.
  - Loss guard: on "Add to catalogue", if the selling price is at/below cost, you get a
      "would sell at a loss — add anyway?" confirm (you can still do a deliberate loss-leader).

Statuses now: new (needs price) → priced → closed (in catalogue) OR rejected (not stocked).
Migration 2026_07_11_000003 adds sourcing_requests.decision_note (the "why not stocked" reason).

STRESS-TEST NOTES (handled)
  - Zero/negative reply price: ignored (must be > 0).  - Convert unpriced/twice: blocked.
  - Below-cost selling price: warned.  - Missing requester phone / unreachable photo: degrades
    to text / skips quietly.  - Reminders are capped (6) and stop on price/closed/rejected.
