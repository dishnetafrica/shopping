IN-APP TASKS — assign work from the phone (answering "how do I do it from V3 / the PWA?")
========================================================================================

Until now, on the phone you could only reach Tasks by opening the menu (☰) → Operations → Tasks, and there
was no first-class way to CREATE/assign a task. This adds that.

WHAT'S NEW (frontend only — reuses the existing /papi/task endpoints)
  • "+" → NEW TASK — a modern sheet: type the task, pick who to assign it to (your staff), set an optional
    due date/time and a priority (Low / Normal / High) → Assign. Posts to /papi/task/assign (the same
    owner/manager-gated endpoint the desktop "New task" uses). Added to the "+" on Today, Customers and
    Operations.
  • OPERATIONS HUB → "Tasks" row (assign & track) so it's one tap from the Operations screen, not buried
    in the menu.

HOW THE WHOLE THING WORKS NOW, END TO END
  1. You (owner/manager) assign a task from the phone: "+" → New Task.
  2. That staff member gets it in their twice-daily WhatsApp digest (9am & 2pm), with their short
     login-free /jobs/{code} link.
  3. They open the link → their "My Jobs" list → tap the task → Accept / Mark done, attaching an optional
     photo — all without logging in.
  4. You see progress in the app: Operations → Tasks (the list updates as they complete).

VERIFY ON A PHONE
  "+" → New Task → type a task, pick a staff member, Assign → "Task assigned". Open Operations → Tasks to
  see it in the list. (If you're not an owner/manager it will say so — the endpoint enforces that.)
