THE FIELD APP  (/panel/my-cash)
  - Shows the logged-in person's own cash in hand (per currency) + recent history with
    running balance. A staff user sees only themselves (server-enforced).
  - Two big buttons, role-tuned: sales/field roles get "I received" as the primary;
    buyers get "I spent". Both always present.
  - Amount-first entry: full-screen number pad, quick chips (10k/50k/…), currency
    defaults to the person's usual currency (from their latest entry), tap to switch.
  - I spent -> an order (picker) OR an expense (category). I received -> customer
    (chips + Walk-in) + optional order. Saves via the same one-entry-two-places engine,
    so orders / customers / P&L update automatically.
  - "Saved ✓" toast, then offers a WhatsApp update to the office. 💬 in the header
    sends the current balance.

NOTE — holder names must match the login name
  My Cash matches entries by the person's user name. If older entries used a different
  spelling for the holder (e.g. "Jayesh Bhai" vs the login "Jayesh"), those won't show
  in that person's My Cash until corrected. Tidy holder names once and it lines up.
