DEPLOY — Payments: CRM-style record form (v21)   [cumulative]
=============================================================
Upgrades the "Record a payment" form to match the CRM style, with a Client picker,
proper Payment-method dropdown, Created date, Note, Send-receipt toggle, and
Invoice/Order no. Builds on v18 (advances + receipts).

NEW / CHANGED
  NEW  database/migrations/2026_07_06_001300_add_invoice_no_to_customer_payments.php
  CHG  app/Models/CustomerPayment.php                (+invoice_no fillable)
  CHG  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/PaymentController.php (invoice_no, created_date
        override, free-text method, send_receipt flag)
  CHG  resources/panel/payments.html                 (CRM-style form)

FORM FIELDS (like the CRM modal)
  - Client: pick from your existing customers (auto-filled from orders) or type a new
    one; picking auto-fills the phone.
  - Amount + Currency.
  - Payment method: Cash / Bank Transfer-ECO Bank / MTN MoMo / Airtel Money /
    Stripe credit card / Other.
  - Type: Advance / On delivery (advance = unearned, shows under "Advances held").
  - Created date: editable (defaults to now) - lets you back-date a payment.
  - Note, Invoice/Order no., optional receipt photo.
  - Send receipt (YES/NO): if YES, the receipt opens right after saving for WhatsApp
    / Print. If NO, it just records.
  Cash still rises by the amount; advances still stay out of invoiced revenue.
