DEPLOY — Credit Notes (CN-2026-0001) — the numbered "we owe you" document
==========================================================================
This mirrors your ISP CRM's "Credit notes" tab. A credit note is the opposite of an invoice:
it records money you owe the customer (a return, a price adjustment, an over-payment or goodwill),
carries its own permanent number (CN-2026-0001), has a branded PDF, can be sent on WhatsApp, and
REDUCES what the customer owes on their statement.

REQUIRES ONE MIGRATION (additive, safe — never wipes data or settings).

SAFE-DEPLOY STEPS
  1. Replace / add the files below -> commit.
  2. php artisan migrate --force        (creates 3 credit-note tables; additive, safe)
  3. EasyPanel REBUILD.
  NEVER run on production: migrate:fresh, migrate:refresh, db:wipe.

WHAT YOU CAN DO NOW
  • Invoices screen → any invoice → "🧾 Credit note": raise a credit note against that invoice
    (defaults to the full balance; type a smaller number for a partial credit; pick a reason).
  • New "Credit notes" register  (/panel/credit-notes, linked from the Invoices screen top bar):

      - "＋ New credit note": pick an invoice to credit OR make a standalone one (over-payment /

  • Customer 360 → new "Credit notes" tab: every credit note for that customer + any credit

  • The customer's own "My account" (shop login) statement shows the credit lines + the reduced
    balance too.

HOW THE ACCOUNTING WORKS (simple + safe)
  • A non-void credit note reduces the customer's outstanding balance on the STATEMENT, per
    currency (USD stays USD, UGX stays UGX). If credits exceed what they owe, the excess shows as
    "Credit in your favour" (store credit you can set off against their next invoice or refund).
  • Numbering is gapless + per-year via its OWN atomic counter (credit_note_counters) — a credit
    note never borrows an invoice number, and two people issuing at once never collide.
  • Voiding a credit note keeps it on record (marked VOID) and stops it reducing the balance.
  • This does NOT touch the cash ledger / unified-payments flow — a credit note is not a cash
    receipt, so it never inflates the cashbook. It only adjusts what is owed.
  • Reasons captured: Return of goods · Price adjustment · Over-payment refund · Goodwill /
    discount · Damaged / short supply · Other.

RELATION TO THE EXISTING RETURN / REFUND FLOW
  The order "↩ Return / refund" (store credit / refund pending / redeem) still works as before for
  quick operational adjustments. Credit notes are the FORMAL numbered document version — issue one
  when the customer needs a proper credit-note PDF or when it must show on the account statement.
