DEPLOY — Staff Cash (slice 1: read-only view) v34
=================================================
The first slice of the Staff Cash system: see each person's "cash in hand" and their
history, built from data you already record (every order_costs row has a holder).
READ-ONLY — nothing new is written, so it can't affect your books. The recording
actions (Give cash / He spent / He received / Settle) come in the next slices.

NEW / CHANGED
  NEW  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/StaffCashController.php
  NEW  resources/panel/staff_cash.html
  CHG  routes/web.php                     (+/panel/staff-cash, +/papi/staff-cash*)
  CHG  resources/panel/app_home.html      (+Staff Cash tile)

WHAT IT SHOWS
  - Company total cash (all hands) at the top, split by person.
  - People list: each person's cash in hand (per currency) + USD equivalent.
  - Tap a person -> their ledger: running balance per currency, every entry marked
    GIVEN / SPENT / RECEIVED, with the "-> also on ORD-xx" link where it ties to an
    order or shows the expense category.
  - "Send balance" button -> opens WhatsApp with their current balance (works now).
  Balance rule (matches Cash Position): money IN = funding / cash_in / customer_payment;
  everything else is money OUT; per currency, using cash actually moved (paid_native).
  Verified: Jayesh +27,000 +2,000,000 -660,000 -527,000 = UGX 840,000 (~$230).

ACCESS
  Owner/manager see everyone; a staff member sees only their own cash (same rule as
  Cash Position). Anyone with the trading panel can open the page.
