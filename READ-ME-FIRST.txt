EA AUTO PART — COMPATIBLE MODELS (authoritative fitment) + all prior hardening
==============================================================================

This adds the proper per-part fitment data model you approved: each part now knows which
motorcycle models it fits, sourced from the OFFICIAL Bajaj SPC catalogues (not guessed).

HOW IT WORKS
------------
A part number that appears in several models' catalogues fits all of them. I scanned the
official catalogues (Boxer 100, Boxer BM125X, BM150, BM150 X, CT 125 HD) and built a
cross-reference: 1,305 part numbers, 676 of which fit more than one model.
  build-data/pnfits_data.json   part number -> [compatible model ids]

The storefront now:
  • Reads this cross-reference (baked into autoparts.html). For any part with a Bajaj part
    number, fitment is AUTHORITATIVE — it fits a bike only if the catalogue says so. No more
    guessing, no false "Not for".
  • Shows a "COMPATIBLE MODELS (N)" panel on the product page (like your EAS Africa mockup),
    with tappable model chips; the customer's own bike is highlighted.
  • Falls back to the smart heuristic (family / universal-hardware) only for parts that have
    no catalogue part number (e.g. some generic supplier items).
  Files: src/03-garage.js (fitsBike, compatibleModels), src/10-views.js (PDP UI),
         resources/storefront/autoparts.html (CSS + baked data), build_storefront.py.

COVERAGE NOTE (important)
  Your full Bajaj catalogue products (which carry part numbers) get authoritative fitment now.
  The ~53 hand-entered supplier items whose keywords have NO Bajaj part number (e.g.
  "6004 Bearing") can't be cross-referenced, so they use the smart heuristic. To make THOSE
  authoritative too, add each one's Bajaj part number to its product keywords and they'll be
  picked up automatically. Send me the supplier part numbers and I'll produce the updated import.
