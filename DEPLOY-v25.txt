DEPLOY — Cashbook: DishNet-style ledger (v25)   [cumulative]
============================================================
Rebuilds the Shree cashbook to match your DishNet ledger: a proper table with
Ref | Date | Particulars | Person | IN | OUT | BALANCE | Status, plus a Category
on each entry and an auto Ref/voucher number.

  NEW  database/migrations/2026_07_06_001400_add_category_to_order_costs.php
  CHG  app/Models/OrderCost.php                              (+category fillable)
  CHG  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/TradeCashbookController.php (category + auto Ref)
  CHG  resources/panel/trade_cashbook.html                    (ledger table + Category field)

WHAT'S NEW (matches DishNet)
  - TABLE with columns: Ref, Date, Particulars (Category + note), Person, IN (green),
    OUT (red), Balance (running, per currency), Status. Green/red left border per row.
    Horizontally scrolls on narrow phones so all columns stay intact.
  - CATEGORY on every entry: choose Receipt / Sale / Funding / Local Purchase /
    Travel & Field / Food Allowance / Transport / Salary / Misc / Other. Auto-derived
    for system entries (Purchase, Receipt, Funding...).
  - AUTO REF per entry: CB-0001 for manual cash in/out; purchases show their ORD-xx;
    others get a stage prefix (FUN-/PAY-/etc.). Traceable like DishNet's SR/voucher.
  - RUNNING BALANCE per currency (from v24) shown in its own column.
  - STATUS shows Done / pending (your cost-approval flow drives this).
