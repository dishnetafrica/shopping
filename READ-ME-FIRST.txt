UPLOAD TO GITHUB — vehicle filter is now clearly OPTIONAL
=========================================================
Picking a vehicle was never required, but now it's obvious:
  • Default bar: "Which motorcycle do you have? … or just browse everything below"
    with a ✕ to hide it.
  • Hidden: a slim line "You're browsing all parts · Pick your motorcycle to filter".
  • After choosing: "Showing parts for <bike> · Change · Show all parts" (one tap to unfilter).
Nothing is gated — customers can shop the whole catalogue without ever choosing a vehicle.

Frontend only (resources/storefront/autoparts.html); PHP unchanged.
