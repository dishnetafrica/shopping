THE MODEL (fits the base+native+rate engine, no new accounting system)
  order_costs gets pay_status (cash|credit) + paid_native (native actually paid).
  - CASH so much as reflects money that LEFT hand -> Cash Position & Cashbook now sum
    paid_native, NOT native_amount.
  - cash purchase:  paid_native = amount   -> reduces cash now.
  - credit purchase: paid_native = 0       -> cash unchanged; becomes supplier debt.
  - "Pay supplier": a stage='supplier_payment' row (paid_native = amount) reduces the
    payer's cash and clears the debt. Excluded from order margin.
  Order margin still uses the full USD cost (usd_amount) -> unaffected by terms.

  Debt(supplier,currency) = Σ credit-purchase native − Σ supplier_payment native.
  Verified end-to-end: float +1,000,000; credit buy 400,000 -> cash STILL 1,000,000,
  owe 400,000; cash buy 150,000 -> cash 850,000; pay 400,000 -> cash 450,000, owe 0.

SAFETY — this touches the LIVE cash formula, so read this:
  - Migration is additive (2 columns) and BACKFILLS paid_native = native_amount on
    every existing row. Because old logic already treated all costs as paid, balances
    DO NOT MOVE on deploy. Only NEW credit purchases behave differently.
  - The model hook defaults paid_native = native for anything not explicitly credit, so
    expenses / cashbook / funding writers needed no change and stay correct.
  - Credit requires a supplier (enforced in the UI) so debt is always attributable.
  - Gated to Shree (order_system_enabled). All additive.

ROLLBACK
  git revert + Rebuild. To drop columns: php artisan migrate:rollback --step=1.
  (Cash reverts to summing native_amount; credit rows would then reduce cash again —
   only relevant if you had booked credit before rolling back.)

KNOWN LIMITS
  - Partial payment of a single purchase isn't modelled per-row; instead record a
    smaller supplier payment — debt reduces by that amount (aggregate). Works fine.
  - If cost_approval is ON, a supplier payment counts to cash after approval (same as
    every other cost). Turn approvals off for purchasing staff if you want it instant.
