DEPLOY — All-in-one: full CRUD (delete added) v42
=================================================
Adds DELETE everywhere it was missing, completing Create/Read/Update/Delete on the
cashbook and receipts. Owner/manager only; deletes are permanent and remove linked
rows together so the books never drift. Single upload — has everything.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
  - Payments Received: each receipt now has a "Delete" button (next to Edit). It removes
    BOTH the payment record AND its mirrored cashbook row, in one transaction. This lets
    you clear duplicates like the two RCP-0000x / Mehul bhai $1,286 rows.
  - Cashbook: the delete (✕) now works on EVERY entry (purchase, funding, receipt, cash
    in/out, expense) — not just cash in/out. Deleting a receipt also removes its linked
    customer-payment record (Customer 360 stays correct).
  - Both are owner/manager only (staff cannot delete). Confirm prompt before deleting;
    hard delete (cannot be undone).
