     invoice_terms_days, credit_terms.

   - Warehouse in Luri: set it on /panel/ads → Bot behaviour; bot tells customers the pickup point.

C) CONSIGNMENT STOCK REQUESTS (Asish → Kampala)           (details: DEPLOY-CONSIGNMENT-REQUEST.txt)
   - The consignment loose-stock is the request→sent→received→sold pipeline (already built).
   - Quick request list: paste "Cling Film 10 / Aluminium Foil 15" → added as Requested.
   - "Send request to Kampala": WhatsApps the short list to Jayesh.
   - One-tap for Asish: a "Request stock from Kampala" card on the consignment list AND a tile on
     the mobile panel — lands on today's open consignment (auto-creates one if none is open).
