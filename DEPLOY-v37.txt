HOW "SETTLE / COUNT" WORKS
  Tap "⚖ Settle" -> pick currency -> the sheet shows what the system thinks they hold
  -> enter the counted amount -> it shows the gap live:
    • Match      -> "Settled ✓", no entry written.
    • Shortage   -> counted < system: records the gap as a 'Shortage' (cash_out,
      category Shortage). Cash in hand drops to the counted amount, and the shortage
      shows as a ⚠ flag on that person until explained/recovered.
    • Overage    -> counted > system: records the gap as an 'Overage' (cash_in) so
      cash in hand rises to the counted amount.
  Verified: system 840,000, counted 820,000 -> shortage 20,000 -> cash in hand = 820,000.
  Owner/manager for anyone; a staff user only for themselves (server-checked).

THE FULL STAFF CASH SYSTEM (all live after this deploy)
