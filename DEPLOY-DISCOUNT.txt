DEPLOY — Order discount ($ or %) (v56)
======================================
Path: resources/panel/seller.html  (the file the app serves)

WHAT'S NEW
  A Discount row in the order invoice totals, between Subtotal and Delivery fee, with a
  toggle:  "$ off" (flat amount) OR "% off" (percentage). Choose per order.
    Total = Subtotal − Discount + Delivery fee
  Use it when you can't deliver the full order or want to give a reduction.

  You can STILL adjust per line (change a line's qty or price) — that already works and is
  the truest way to handle a short-delivered item. The discount field is for a flat/percent
  reduction on top.

OUTSTANDING IS CORRECT
  The discounted total is saved as the order total, so the Record Payment modal shows
  Outstanding = discounted total automatically. Pay the discounted amount and the order
  clears.

HOW IT PERSISTS (no backend change needed)
  The discount reduces the saved order total. On reload the app reconstructs it:
    saved total < subtotal  -> the difference is shown as Discount
    saved total > subtotal  -> the difference is shown as Delivery fee (unchanged behaviour)
  LIMITATION: applying BOTH a manual delivery fee AND a discount on the SAME order will,
  after a reload, show as a single combined adjustment (the math/total stays correct; only
  the split between "discount" and "delivery" may merge). For a clean separate discount that
  always survives reload, I can add a proper discount field to the order backend — send me
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/PanelApiController.php (the saveOrder method) and I'll wire it.

DEPLOY
  1. Upload to  resources/panel/seller.html  (REPLACE existing). Commit.
  2. EasyPanel REBUILD (force/no-cache).
  3. Container: grep -c tDiscMode /var/www/html/resources/panel/seller.html  (>0)
     then php /var/www/html/artisan view:clear
  4. Normal browser: clear service worker + caches, reopen panel.

VERIFY
  Open an order -> Discount row shows under Subtotal. Enter "50" with "$ off" -> Total drops
  by 50. Switch to "% off", enter 10 -> Total = Subtotal − 10%. Save. Record Payment ->
  Outstanding equals the discounted Total.
