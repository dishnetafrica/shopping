THIS UPDATE
-----------
1) FITMENT BUG FIXED (the "Not for your CT 125 HD" on every part).
   Root cause: your supplier stock is tagged generically (e.g. "BOXER | Engine | 6004 BEARING"),
   but the old check demanded an exact match to the selected model, so CT 125 HD matched nothing
   and the code fell back to showing everything stamped "Not for your CT 125 HD".
   New behaviour (src/03-garage.js, src/04-vehicle.js): smart 3-state fitment —
     • FITS  — the model or its family (BOXER/CT/…) is named, OR it's universal/generic hardware
               (bearing, bolt, seal, cable, filter, chain, sprocket…). Shows "✓ Fits your …".
     • DIFFERENT MODEL — only when the part clearly names another specific model. Subtle badge.
     • UNKNOWN — shows NO scary badge; part stays visible.
   Lists now hide only confident non-fits, so generic stock is no longer wrongly rejected.

2) LEFT CATEGORY SIDEBAR (from your EAS Africa mockup) on the Parts / system / search pages.
   Categories with live counts + active highlight, plus "In stock only" and "Fits my bike"
   filters. Collapses to chips on mobile. (src/09-shell.js, src/04-vehicle.js, src/10-views.js
   + CSS in resources/storefront/autoparts.html.)

NOTE ON DEEPER FIX: the generic tagging ("BOXER") is a DATA issue. The smart fitment copes with
it well, but the best long-term fix is to tag each supplier part with the models it actually
fits (a "Compatible Models" list, like your mockup). Happy to build that import once you decide.
