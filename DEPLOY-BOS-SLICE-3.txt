DEPLOY — BOS v1.0 Slice 3: Today (the day's work, on your phone)
================================================================
The mobile "Today" tab stops being the old briefing and becomes the day's work: a greeting, four
KPI tiles (To Collect / Overdue / Quotes Waiting / Collected Today), and a ranked "To do now" list of
who to chase — each row one tap into that customer's Timeline (Slice 2). No new data: Today groups the
same live invoices/payments/quotes the timeline is already built from. Every number traces to a real
query — nothing invented (R23, ADR-006). Desktop is untouched (still lands on the classic briefing).

Prereq: Slice 1 (bos_events migrated) + Slice 2 (customer timeline) already deployed. No migration here.

FILES CHANGED
  app/BOS/Projections/CollectionsActivities.php   (NEW) Activity projection — one "Collect USD X — name"
                                                        per customer who owes, ranked by value x urgency.
  app/BOS/Projections/DailyToday.php              (NEW) Today projection — greeting + 4 KPIs + activities.
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/BosTodayController.php (NEW) GET /papi/bos-today — pure read, never throws.
  routes/web.php                                  +1 line: the /papi/bos-today route (in the papi group,
                                                        right after receipt-send). Nothing else changed.
  resources/views/shell.blade.php                 + the Today screen (bosToday) + tone-accented KPI/CSS
                                                        + route guard; mobile "Today" tab now opens it;
                                                        phone landing goes to Today (desktop unchanged).

HOW IT WORKS (all overlay, all reversible)
  - Opening the app on a phone (or tapping the Today tab) calls GET /papi/bos-today, which runs the
    DailyToday projection: SUM of open invoice balances (To Collect), overdue count, quotes waiting,
    and today's receipts — all live queries. The activity list is CollectionsActivities: overdue-first,
    biggest-first. Tapping a row opens that customer's Timeline.
  - The controller is wrapped in try/catch and returns ok:false (HTTP 200) on any error, so a bad day
    view can never white-screen the app — it just shows "Today unavailable".
  - Pure read end to end: no writes, no rule logic, no new table. Fitness stays green.

VERIFY
  1. On your phone, open the app -> you land on "Good morning, <name>" with the four tiles and the
     ranked collect list. Tap a row -> the customer's Timeline opens.
  2. The tiles are real: "To Collect" equals the sum of open invoice balances; "Collected Today" equals
     today's receipts (both in USD). Cross-check against Finance if you like.
  3. Empty-state: on a tenant with nothing outstanding, the list shows "All clear" and tiles read 0.
  4. Desktop unchanged: on a computer the app still opens the classic briefing; the "Today" label in the
     sidebar behaves exactly as before.
  5. Safety: if the projection ever errors, you see "Today unavailable" — never a broken screen; the
     money paths (invoices/payments/receipts) are completely untouched.
