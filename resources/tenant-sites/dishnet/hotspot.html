How it works

Four steps to start

From your first order to your first paying customer — typically two weeks.

1

Order your connection

Pick Fiber if you're in Juba, Starlink Priority anywhere else. We'll match the plan tier to how many users you expect.

2

We install & configure

DishNet handles physical setup and configures the hotspot system — voucher-based access, user limits, dashboard. No technical knowledge needed from you.

3

Sell internet access

You sell daily, weekly, or monthly vouchers to neighbors, tenants, or walk-in customers. Cash or mobile money — your choice.

4

Monitor & grow

See active users, revenue, and usage from your phone. DishNet provides ongoing support and helps you upgrade as you scale.

Starlink generally provides reliable service in South Sudan, but no internet is 100%. If the connection drops, your hotspot pauses automatically — paid users aren't billed for unavailable time. Uptime is Starlink's responsibility, not DishNet's.
For a small hotspot (under 10 users), the 20 Mbps plan at {{price:Fiber Basic 20 Mbps}}/mo works fine. For 10–20 users or anywhere you'll have concurrent video calls, go with 40 Mbps ({{price:Fiber Standard 40 Mbps}}/mo). For an office building or larger setup, 60 Mbps ({{price:Fiber Premium 60 Mbps}}/mo) is the right choice. You can upgrade between tiers as you grow.
