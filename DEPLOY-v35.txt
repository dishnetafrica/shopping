DEPLOY — Staff Cash slice 2: Give cash + WhatsApp confirm (v35)
==============================================================
Adds the first WRITE action: give cash to a person. It records a funding entry on
that person's account (their cash in hand goes up), then offers to WhatsApp them a
confirmation. Owner/manager only. Builds on slice 1 (v34).

FILES CHANGED
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/StaffCashController.php   (+give())
  routes/web.php                                        (+POST staff-cash/give)
  resources/panel/staff_cash.html                       (Give cash form + WhatsApp)

HOW "GIVE CASH" WORKS
  - Tap "＋ Give cash" -> choose the person, amount, currency (rate auto-fills for
    UGX/SSP, editable), how (Cash / MoMo / Airtel / Bank), optional note, date.
  - Saves as a 'funding' entry (holder = that person, category "Funding / Float",
    status approved). Their cash in hand rises immediately; it shows in their ledger
    as GIVEN and in the company total.
  - After saving, it offers to open WhatsApp to send them: "Given you <amount>,
    please confirm you received it" — the trust step.
  - Only owner/manager can give cash (checked server-side).

NOTE: since you fund people independently, Give cash only adds to the receiver; it
does not reduce anyone else. (If later you want it to also come out of Bhavin's
hand, that's a one-line change to write a linked pair.)
