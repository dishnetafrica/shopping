DEPLOY — Cash Position sign fix (v49)
=====================================
CRITICAL data-accuracy fix. The Cash Position screen was showing every balance with the
WRONG SIGN — money you HOLD was displayed as NEGATIVE.

ROOT CAUSE
  Cash Position treated only stages ['funding','customer_payment'] as cash-IN. It was
  missing 'cash_in' — the stage used by currency conversions and moved cash. So conversion
  inflows (e.g. Jayesh UGX 4,700,330, Bhavin UGX 2,193,000) were signed as OUT, flipping
  the whole screen negative (showed UGX -6,893,330 / combined -$3,086 instead of
  +6,893,330 / +$1,886).

  Every other money screen (Cashbook, Staff Cash, My Cash) already used the correct rule
  ['funding','cash_in','customer_payment'] — Cash Position was the only one out of step.
  This is why the verification showed all screens agreeing EXCEPT Cash Position.

FIX (one line, CashPositionController::data())
  $sign = in_array($r->stage,['funding','cash_in','customer_payment'],true) ? 1 : -1;
  (added 'cash_in' so it matches the cashbook exactly)
