Your shop — quick guide

What each part of your panel does, and how to use it. Search below, or just scroll.

🤖 Your WhatsApp assistant

Answers your customers on WhatsApp around the clock — shares your catalogue, gives prices, takes orders, and sends quotations, so you don’t have to be online for every message.

You stay in control: jump into any chat whenever you want.

📋 Today — what needs you

Open: Today

Your starting screen each day. It lists what needs action now and gives you one button for each — open orders, quotes awaiting a reply, deliveries to confirm, stock to count, and customers worth a nudge. When there’s nothing pending, it says “All clear”.

📦 Orders

Open: Orders

See every order and move it along as you work it — accept, prepare, dispatch, deliver. Your customer is kept informed automatically along the way.

📄 Quotations

Open: Quotations

Send customers a professional price quote. Tap View on any quote to:

  • Adjust quantities or prices and update it.
  • Resend it, turn it into an order, or archive it once it’s done.

📊 Stock counts

Open: Today → Count, or Stock count

Tell your shop how much you have of each product. Counted items show customers a clear availability; type the quantity and press Save.

  • Use “Needs a count only” to quickly find what to update.
  • Keep the fast movers up to date so customers always get accurate answers.

🔁 Lead follow-up

Turn on: Settings → Lead follow-up

When a customer asks about something but doesn’t come back, your shop can send your catalogue to gently remind them — so warm leads don’t slip away.

  • Switch it on, choose how long to wait, and pick the catalogue to send.

👥 Customers

Open: Customers

A record of everyone who messages you, with notes and contact details — handy for repeat orders and a more personal service.

🛒 Products & catalogue

Open: Products

Your product list is the heart of your shop — names, prices, and photos. Add items one by one or import a list in bulk. Keep prices current so every quote is right.

🚚 Delivery confirmation

After an order is delivered, your customer gets a link to confirm they received it and leave a rating — giving you a clean record and useful feedback.

✋ Jumping into a chat

Whenever you reply to a customer yourself, your assistant steps aside so you’re both not answering at once — and picks back up when you’re done.

⚙️ Settings

Open: Settings

Set your shop details, prices and currency, delivery charges, and how your assistant replies (product photos, suggestions, lead follow-up). Adjust to suit your business any time.

💬 Need a hand?

If something doesn’t look right, contact your account manager — mention the order or quote number and what you expected, and we’ll sort it quickly.
