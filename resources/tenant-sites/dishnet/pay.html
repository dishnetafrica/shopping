Protect yourself from payment scams

DishNet will never do any of the following. If anyone claiming to be from DishNet does, stop and call our office on +211 923 400 000.

  • Ask you to send mobile money to a personal phone number
  • Ask you for your bank password, full card details, or one-time codes (OTPs)
  • Send bank details from a number other than +211 921 443 009
  • Pressure you to pay immediately to "avoid disconnection" — real reminders give you days, not minutes
  • Ask you to install remote-access software on your phone or computer
