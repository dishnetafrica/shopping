Which battery? AGM vs LiFePO4

The battery is the heart of backup — and the cheaper one is often the costlier choice.

AGM / Lead-acid

Lower upfront
Lifespan2–4 years
Usable capacity~50%
MaintenanceSome
WeightHeavy
Best forTight budgets
BEST VALUE

LiFePO4 (lithium)

Best over its life
Lifespan8–10 years
Usable capacity~80–90%
MaintenanceNone
WeightLight
Best forLong-term value

Over its life, LiFePO4 usually costs less per year — it lasts longer, gives more usable energy, and needs no upkeep. Read the full battery guide →
