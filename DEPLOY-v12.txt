DEPLOY — Profit share by ownership % (v12)   [cumulative]
=========================================================
Adds "Distribute profit by %" on the Partners screen. Enter the profit to share;
it splits by each partner's ownership % into their capital account. Owner-only.

NEW / CHANGED for v12
  CHG  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/PartnerController.php   (+distribute())
  CHG  routes/web.php                                     (+/papi/partners/distribute)
  CHG  resources/panel/partners.html                      (Distribute UI + live preview)
  (v11 partners tables/models must already be deployed + migrated)

HOW IT WORKS
  - Owner taps "Distribute profit by %" -> enters profit amount (USD) + note.
  - It tries to prefill the amount from /papi/pnl (net profit); edit if needed.
  - Live preview shows each partner's share = amount x their% / total% (exact,
    last partner absorbs the rounding remainder). Verified: 50/30/20 of $1000 ->
    500/300/200; equal 3-way of $100 -> 33.33/33.33/33.34.
  - Saving writes a partner_capital row (kind 'profit_share') per partner.

IMPORTANT — profit share does NOT move cash
  Opening/introduced/drawings move cash (they did in v11). Profit distribution is
  DIFFERENT: it's retained earnings moving INTO capital accounts. The cash is
  already in the business from operations, so distribute() writes NO cash row.
  => After distributing, Total Capital will be HIGHER than Cash on Hand. That's
     correct accounting (the gap = profit earned, held as goods/cash/receivables).
     Only the cash-injection kinds reconcile 1:1 with cash.
