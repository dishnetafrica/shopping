WEB-ROOT ASSETS — important
  The 4 files under public/ must sit at the site web root so the icon + manifest
  resolve at /apple-touch-icon.png, /icon-192.png, /icon-512.png, /site.webmanifest.
  In Laravel that's the project's public/ folder (already web root). If your web
  root is elsewhere (aaPanel/EasyPanel), drop them there.
