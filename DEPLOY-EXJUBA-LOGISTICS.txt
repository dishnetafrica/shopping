DEPLOY — Ex-Juba pricing wording + "learn the customer's route" (South Sudan)
=============================================================================

WHAT CHANGED
1) PRICES ARE EX-JUBA (not ex-Kampala).
   The bot + quotation PDF now say the USD price is "ex-<your basis city>" and that onward
   transport to the customer's own town is arranged AFTER the order (not in the quote). Set your
   basis city to Juba on /panel/ads → Bot behaviour → "Price basis city" = Juba.

2) SOUTH SUDAN = MORE THAN JUBA.
   The bot now explicitly knows Wau, Malakal, Bor, Yei, Aweil, Bentiu, Rumbek, Torit, Kuajok,
   Renk, Nimule, Nasir, Maridi, Yambio, etc. are South Sudan — so a customer from any of these is
   priced in USD ex-Juba, and it never invents a free delivery to their town.

3) LEARN THEIR ROUTE (acts like a real salesperson).
   Once items are agreed and the customer is NOT in Juba, the bot asks ONE short question about how
   they normally receive goods — e.g. "Do you have a transporter/route you use from Juba, or will
   you send someone to collect from our Juba store?" — then:
     - if they have a transporter/route → tells them we'll hand the goods to it in Juba on their
       behalf,
     - if they'll collect → confirms the Juba pickup point,
   and ALWAYS records their delivery method / transporter / contact in the ORDER note, so Asish and
   the team can arrange the onward leg. It doesn't force a delivery fee — their route sets that.

SET IT UP  (/panel/ads → "Bot behaviour")
   - "Price basis city" = Juba.
   - (Warehouse / pickup location already set, e.g. Luri, is what the bot points collectors to.)
   Optional: settings.quote_terms overrides the quotation footer wording entirely if you want your
   own; settings.ssMarkupPct is the South Sudan markup applied to the USD price.
