DEPLOY — Buy List window fix (v13)
==================================
Bug: an order that advanced to "Out for delivery" (e.g. ORD-3) without its items
being ticked "bought" still showed on the Buy List. Fix: items now leave the Buy
List once the order is Out for delivery / Delivered / Cancelled.

CHANGE (one line, front-end)
  resources/panel/buy_list.html
    WINDOW = ['Confirmed','Packed']   (was ['Confirmed','Packed','Out for delivery'])

EFFECT
  - Buy List shows items only while the order is Confirmed or Packed.
  - The moment an order goes Out for delivery/Delivered/Cancelled, its items drop
    off automatically (ORD-3 disappears).
  - Ready-to-pack is unaffected (it was already Confirmed-only).
  Verified: ORD-3 (Out for delivery) excluded; Confirmed/Packed orders still show.

  Upload to resources/panel/buy_list.html -> commit -> Rebuild. No migration.

  Open /panel/buy-list -> ORD-3's foil & cling are gone. A Confirmed order still
