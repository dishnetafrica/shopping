DEPLOY — WhatsApp bot fixes from real chats (SkyTrips, MOQ, product guidance,
         warehouse in Luri, and "don't stop the AI when I message by hand")
=============================================================================

WHAT THESE FIX (all seen in your live chats)

1) "SkyTrips" — the #1 bug.
   Customers arriving from your Facebook page/ad "SkyTrips" were getting:
     "Sorry, I don't have information on 'SkyTrips'…"
   Cause: the ad's TITLE ("SkyTrips") is your PAGE/CAMPAIGN name, but the bot treated it as a
   PRODUCT and searched for it — so it apologised AND ignored the customer's real question
   ("What is the minimum order quantity?").
   Fix: a new "ad page/brand names to ignore" list. When the ad title matches one of these
   (or your own business name), the bot skips it and just answers the customer normally.
   → Set it on /panel/ads → "Bot behaviour" → add:  SkyTrips
   (Your business name is always ignored automatically; you can list several, comma-separated.)

2) "What is the minimum order quantity?" now gets a real answer.
   Once the SkyTrips pollution is gone, this reaches the bot cleanly. The bot now answers with a
   wholesale default: "We sell wholesale by the carton — minimum 1 carton. Tell me which item and
   I'll confirm its carton size, minimum and price." (In AI mode the prompt now says the same.)
   You can override the wording on /panel/ads → "Minimum-order note".

3) Guide customers on what things are FOR.
   When listing products (e.g. disposable glasses), the bot now adds a short use note —
   "good for juice, water or soft drinks", "foil for wrapping & takeaway trays", etc. It uses each
   product's own description when set, otherwise sensible real-world knowledge. (AI mode: new rule
   "GUIDE, DON'T JUST LIST" + the product's description is now fed into the catalogue the AI sees.)
   TIP: fill in a product's Description to control the exact use note it gives.

4) Warehouse in Luri.
   Set your pickup point once on /panel/ads → "Warehouse / pickup location", e.g.
     Luri, just outside Juba, South Sudan
   Now when a customer asks "where are you / can I collect / do you have a warehouse", the bot
   tells them the Luri pickup point (both the AI brain and the inbuilt bot).
   (For reference: Luri is a real locality on the western edge of Juba, South Sudan — so customers
   can indeed Google it. See sources in the chat.)

5) "Even if we send one message by hand, don't stop the AI."
   Before: any manual WhatsApp reply from your number paused the bot for ~60 min (so a human could
   take over). A stray message therefore silenced the AI.
   Now: a new switch "Keep AI replying after I message a customer" (/panel/ads → Bot behaviour).
   Turn it ON → your manual message is still saved so the AI sees it for context, but the AI keeps
   answering immediately (no pause). Turn it OFF → old behaviour (manual message pauses the bot).
   Note: the web-inbox "Take over" button is unchanged — that's a deliberate takeover and still
   silences the bot for that chat until you hand it back.

6) PHOTOS — recognise what customers send, and send our own product photos.
   INBOUND (customer sends a photo): the "SkyTrips" fix is what unblocks this. In the failing chat
   the customer sent a Galaxy Pack photo + "What is the MOQ?", but the ad title "SkyTrips" was
   injected as the product, so the AI answered "I don't have SkyTrips" and ignored the image. With
   SkyTrips in the ignore list, the real question + the photo reach the AI (vision), which reads
   the box and matches it. So: add SkyTrips to the ignore list (item 1) and photo questions work.
   (Photo vision needs your OpenAI key + feature_image_search on — both already set.)
   OUTBOUND (bot sends a product photo): the bot already sends a product's photo when a customer
   asks or names a product. NEW: it now also sends the photo of the product the BOT recommended —
   e.g. customer says "cup for juice", the bot suggests *Galaxy Pack Disposable Glasses 500ml* and
   the photo goes with it (only when the reply names exactly one product, so lists don't spam).
   REQUIREMENT: the product must have a PHOTO uploaded. New "⚠ missing photo" finder on Products
   (next to "missing pack size") lists products with no image and flags each card — fill those so
   the bot always has a photo to send. (Governed by the send_product_images setting, on by default.)
