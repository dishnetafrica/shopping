DEPLOY — Product cost + margin (profit view) (v58)
==================================================
Built against your CURRENT code. Adds a per-product COST PRICE and a live margin/profit
readout. NO stock tracking changes, NO tax — exactly the light "profit view" scope.

WHAT'S NEW
  - Product Add & Edit forms now have a "Cost price" field next to Price.
  - As you type cost + price it shows live:  Profit: <amount>  ·  Margin: <%>
    (margin = (price − cost) / cost × 100, i.e. markup on cost, like Ultimate POS).
  - Cost is saved per product and returned by the products API (so it prefills on edit and is
    available for profit reporting later).

FILES (correct served paths)
  database/migrations/2026_07_09_000001_add_cost_price_to_products.php   (adds products.cost_price)
  app/Models/Product.php                                                (fillable + cast)
  app/Http/Controllers/Panel/PanelApiController.php                     (add/update/list handle cost)
  resources/panel/seller.html                                          (Cost field + live margin)
       ^ NOTE: this is the file the app actually serves (confirmed on your container).

DEPLOY
  1. Upload all four at those paths (REPLACE existing). Commit.
  2. php artisan migrate            (adds the cost_price column)
  3. EasyPanel REBUILD (force/no-cache).
  4. Normal browser: clear service worker + caches, reopen the panel.

VERIFY
  - Products → edit any item → a "Cost price" field appears; type a cost → Profit & Margin
    show live. Save, reopen → cost is still there.
  - Add product → Cost field + margin readout works the same.

WHAT THIS IS / ISN'T (so expectations are clear)
  - This is SLICE 1: cost & margin captured per product, shown in the product form.
  - It does NOT yet change the "Orders & Margin" screen. That screen still computes margin
    from booked order_costs (what you actually spent). Feeding product cost_price into an
    order/period profit report is SLICE 2 — say the word and I'll add it as a separate,
    clearly-labelled "gross margin from product cost" lens (kept separate so we never
    double-count product cost against booked purchase costs).
  - No stock levels, no VAT (as you chose).
