HOW IT WORKS:
  - Every cost booked in NATIVE currency + rate → stores USD base (DishNet's model).
  - stage=funding ADDS cash to a holder; purchase/freight/delivery REDUCE it.
  - Cash Position shows exact USD / UGX / SSP in hand, per holder (Jaeysh, Asish...).
  - Rates default from tenant settings rate_ugx_per_usd / rate_ssp_per_usd (override per entry).
