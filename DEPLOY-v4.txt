BUY LIST
  - Orders are now entered item-by-item (name + qty + unit) -> trade_order_items.
    (Old free-text still accepted; legacy orders just won't appear in the list.)
  - /panel/buy-list totals every still-unbought item across all orders, grouped by
    name (case-insensitive), showing remaining qty + which orders + a "Bought" button.
    Verified: "Router" 2 + "router" 1 merge to 3; fully-bought rows drop off; a
    partly-bought line (Sugar 30, bought 10) shows remaining 20.
  - Nav: a "List" tab appears for purchasing staff (has 'purchases' menu, not owner).
    Owner/manager reach it via the "Today's buy list" button on Orders & Margin.

SUPPLIERS
  - suppliers table (unique per tenant) + supplier_id/supplier_name on order_costs.
  - Book Purchase has a Supplier picker with a "+" quick-add (works for single AND split).
    supplier_id rides along on every cost row -> per-supplier spend later.

SAFETY
  - All additive: 2 new tables + 2 nullable columns; no existing column touched.
  - Gated to Shree via order_system_enabled.
  - quickOrder stays backward compatible (JSON items -> structured; else free text).
  - Single-order booking flow unchanged; supplier is optional everywhere.
