THE MODEL (advance = liability, not revenue)
  Recording a payment does THREE things atomically:
    1. Cash on hand goes UP (mirrored order_costs stage 'customer_payment', money in).
    2. A customer_payments record is kept (receipt, method, ref, optional photo).
    3. If it's an ADVANCE it's UNEARNED -> shows in "Advances held", and does NOT
       count as invoiced revenue (revenue still only at Delivered).
  Tie a payment to an order (ORD-xx) OR leave it as account credit -- both allowed.
  Verified: 2 advances ($100 + UGX 365,500@3655=$100) -> Advances held $200; cash +$250
  incl. a $50 on-delivery payment; advances excluded from invoiced revenue.

RECEIPTS
  After saving, a receipt opens: business, receipt no. (RCP-xxxxx), date, customer,
  amount (native + USD), method, what it's for. Two buttons:
    - WhatsApp: opens WhatsApp to the customer's number with the receipt text
      (or the contact picker if no number).
    - Print / PDF: opens a clean printable receipt (Save as PDF on the print dialog).
