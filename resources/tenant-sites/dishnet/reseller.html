Why partner with us

What you get as a reseller

Same program whether you sell fiber, satellite, or both.

Better wholesale pricing

Discount on hardware orders above minimum quantity. Pricing scales with volume — the more you sell, the better your margin.

Requirements

What we look for in a reseller

Same criteria whether you're selling fiber or satellite.

  • A physical presence in your area — shop, office, or established business location. Customers need somewhere to find you.
  • Basic reachability — WhatsApp, working phone, ability to respond to inbound enquiries within a day.
  • Initial commitment — a minimum starter order to qualify for the program. We'll discuss what makes sense for your market.
  • Honest customer handling — accurate plan recommendations, no overpromising on speeds or coverage, fair pricing.
  • Knowledge of your area — you understand local demand, what customers can afford, and where the opportunities are.
  • Compliance with operator terms — for satellite resellers, respect upstream service terms. For fiber resellers, work within DishNet's coverage areas.
