DEPLOY — Full Purchase (product-line) + richer suppliers (v59)
=============================================================
Built ON your existing foundations (Supplier model, order_costs, supplier-debt), so purchases
flow into Cash Position, P&L, ledger and "Suppliers We Owe" automatically — no parallel system.

NEW SCREEN:  /panel/purchases
  - Pick a supplier (or add one inline: name, phone, pay-term days, opening balance).
  - Add product line items: product (autocomplete from your catalogue) + qty + unit cost.
    Unit cost pre-fills from the product's saved cost if it has one. Line & grand totals live.
  - Currency (UGX/USD/SSP) + rate.
  - Payment:
      CASH  -> pick "whose cash" (Bhavin/Asish/Jayesh); reduces that person's cash in hand.
      CREDIT-> adds to that supplier's balance in "Suppliers We Owe" (supplier required).
  - On save each line becomes an order_costs row (stage 'purchase') sharing one
    purchase_group_id, and each product's COST PRICE is updated (converted to your UGX base),
    so margins stay current everywhere.

SUPPLIERS enriched: pay_term_days, opening_balance (+ currency). Opening balances are folded
into "Suppliers We Owe".

WHAT'S DELIBERATELY NOT INCLUDED (your choices): no stock quantity tracking, no VAT/tax.

DEPLOY
  1. Upload all at those paths (REPLACE existing). Commit.
  2. php artisan migrate
  3. EasyPanel REBUILD (force/no-cache).
  4. Normal browser: clear service worker + caches. Open  mycloudbss.com/panel/purchases
     (add a sidebar link later if you want; the page works directly by URL now).

VERIFY

    total updates. Choose CREDIT -> Record -> check "Suppliers We Owe" shows the amount.
  - Do a CASH purchase attributed to Asish -> Cash Position shows Asish reduced.
  - Open one of those products -> its Cost price now reflects the purchase unit cost, and the
    Profit/Margin in the edit form updates.
