====================================================================================

THE SHORT ANSWER TO "how do we handle Asish requesting Cling Film 10, Aluminium 15?"
  Use the CONSIGNMENT loose-stock — it is ALREADY a request → sent → received → sold pipeline.
  Asish's request is just loose-stock lines with a REQUESTED quantity. Jayesh fulfils by setting
  SENT when he loads them; on arrival Asish sets RECEIVED; then SOLD as he sells in the market.
  Anything requested-but-not-sent shows as a red "short" and can be carried to the next
  consignment. So you do NOT need a separate system — this is what it's for.

WHAT WAS MISSING (now added) — two conveniences so it matches how Asish actually asks:

1) QUICK REQUEST LIST (paste)
   On the consignment screen, under Loose stock, there's a new box:
       "Quick request list — paste what Asish needs, one item per line"
   Paste exactly what he sends, e.g.
       Cling Film 10
       Aluminium Foil 15
   Tap "＋ Add all as requested". Each line is parsed (name + quantity) and matched to a product
   from your list, then added as a loose-stock line with REQUESTED = that quantity (cost/price
   auto-filled from the product). Accepts "Cling Film 10", "Cling Film x10", "10 Cling Film",
   "Cling Film - 10". Anything it can't match is still added as a loose item and listed so you can
   fix the name.

2) SEND REQUEST TO KAMPALA (WhatsApp)
   Button "📤 Send request to Kampala" WhatsApps the still-short list (requested > sent) to the
   Kampala dispatcher so Jayesh knows what to pack:
       📋 Stock request — CNS-00012 (Kampala → Juba)
       Please pack & send:
       • 10 × Cling Film
       • 15 × Aluminium Foil
       Mark each as Sent on the consignment once loaded. — Family Shoppers
   Target number = setting consignment_dispatch_phone, else your sourcing buyer number
   (sourcing_buyer_phone = Jayesh) — so it works out of the box if that's already set.

THE DAILY FLOW (recommended)
   1. Asish sends his list on WhatsApp → you paste it into "Quick request list" on TODAY's
      consignment → Add all as requested.
   2. Tap "Send request to Kampala" → Jayesh gets the pack list.
   3. Jayesh packs and sets SENT on each line (or edits the number if he sends part).
   4. Dispatch the consignment as usual (one AWB). On arrival Asish sets RECEIVED, then SELL.
   5. Shortfalls (requested > sent) carry to the next consignment via "Carry over unsent requests".
