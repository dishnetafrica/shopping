EA AUTO PART — 3 more Boxer catalogues (BM125X, BM150, BM150 X)
================================================================
Extracted verbatim from the official Bajaj Spare Parts Catalogues you shared.
Every part number was checked back against the source PDF: 100% match (0 errors).

WHAT'S HERE
  products.csv          1,654 products across 3 models, columns:
                        Name, SKU, Category, Price, Cost, Stock, MOQ, Unit, Image, Keywords
  images/               135 exploded-diagram pictures (45 plates x 3 models)
  diagrams-json/        boxer125x.json / boxer150.json / boxer150x.json
                        (structured plate + part data — for the exploded-diagram view)

COUNTS
  Boxer BM125X   45 plates   517 unique parts
  Boxer BM150    45 plates   588 unique parts
  Boxer BM150 X  45 plates   549 unique parts

HOW TO LOAD THE PRODUCTS
  In the app:  Products -> Import products -> upload THIS zip.
  The importer reads products.csv and attaches each part's plate diagram
  (the Image column names a file inside images/).

BEFORE SELLING — set your prices
  Price and Cost are intentionally left blank. Fill Cost with your supplier
  price and set Price (or use your markup %) so margins are correct. Stock is
  blank too — set it for your fast movers.

NOTE — "Boxer 125 HD"
  There is NO spare-parts catalogue for the 125 HD — only a 2-page marketing
  brochure. BM125X is the 125cc parts catalogue (many parts are shared). If your
  125 HD needs its own parts list, we'd need Bajaj's 125 HD SPC PDF.

This zip is for IMPORT — it does NOT go to GitHub.
